Frequently asked questions

Clear answers at the point of decision — another HCI principle that keeps the interface understandable.

How do I book a doctor?
Search by specialty, doctor, condition or city, open a doctor profile, choose online or in-clinic consultation, select a slot and confirm the appointment.
Can I have a video consultation?
Yes. Eligible doctors show an online consultation option. After booking, the patient can join the secure consultation room from the dashboard.
Can I book a lab test from home?
Yes. Select a test and laboratory, choose home sample collection when available, provide the address and confirm the order.
What can doctors manage from their dashboard?
Doctors can manage schedules, appointments, patient records, consultations, prescriptions, follow-ups, availability, reviews and earnings.
Is my medical information private?
The UI is designed around secure access, role-based views, clear consent and minimal exposure of sensitive information.
